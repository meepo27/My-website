Throughout the pages of this chapter, you will find some good practices for each stage of the AutoML process:

  1. Feature engineering
     1. Data source split

As a general rule, a full AutoML usage (going through all three stages in one go) is preferable when you want to build your first model. In this case, AutoML serves as a kickstart to your data science loop.

When looking at AutoML as a whole, always try to run it first with a very aggressive sample of your data. The model results wonât be useful for any deployment, but you will be able to âbenchmarkâ how much time the tasks take. More importantly, you will be able to understand if your job profiles are configured correctly for this task: if using a random sample of 1% of your data only works with 1 GB of memory per executor to run, then the full data is expected to take at most 100 GB to run safely.

From the user perspective, most of the effort should be put into creating a good semantic file. Analyze your data, understand data skewness and relationships between variables. With that in mind you can avoid downstream issues in AutoML.
