# Manager Guide

This guide helps you understand how you can use Case Manager to manage your team and detect suspicious activity more efficiently. As a manager, your main responsibilities can be divided into the tasks described in this page.

## Monitor performanceâ

Leverage dashboards and reports to efficiently monitor the performance of your team as well as the system.

### [Analyze MetricsUse the different dashboards and metrics to monitor how your team and the system are performing.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/analyzing-metrics)

### [Manage ReportsCapture a snapshot of the performance during a given timeframe based on the information from the available dashboards.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-reports)

## Manage workloadâ

Manage your team's workload according to your business needs.

### Automate processesâ

The high flux of events constantly reaching Case Manager makes it difficult to manually distribute the workload in a timely manner. Case Manager includes various tools that allow you to automate how events and cases are organized and distributed.

### [Manage Automation RulesAutomatically process events and cases according to the specified attributes.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-automation-rules)

### [Manage SLAsAutomatically process events and cases according to a time-period, ensuring that financial institutions comply with time-sensitive review policies.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-slas)

### Organize workload manuallyâ

Even with automated processes in place, some situations may require you to manually adjust how the workload is distributed. For example, you may want to manually add an alert to a queue that represents a new pattern, or assign an alert that requires a specific skill set to a given analyst.

### [Assign WorkManually assign alerts and cases to analysts.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/assigning-alerts-and-cases)

### [Manage Access GroupsEnsure that alerts are only visible to the analysts who should have access to them.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-access-groups)

### [Manage CasesGroup related events that require further investigation.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-cases)

### [Manage QueuesSegregate alerts and cases according to the criteria that better fits your needs (e.g. priority, region, business line, etc).](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-queues)

## Manage system configurationsâ

Improve the configurations available to analysts when investigating suspicious behavior.

### [Manage ReasonsSet up the reasons used by analysts when classifying alerts.](/case-manager/13.17/docs/common/case-manager/user-guide/manager-guide/managing-reasons)
