The H2O XGBoost configuration object used for model training.

Parameters:

Name | Type | Description | Default  
---|---|---|---  
  
###  `reg_alpha` Â¶

|  `float` |  L1 regularization. |  `0.0`  
  
###  `max_bins` Â¶

|  `int` |  For tree_method=hist only: maximum number of bins. |  `256`  
  
###  `dmatrix_type` Â¶

|  `str` |  Type of DMatrix. For sparse, NAs and 0 are treated equally. |  `'auto'`  
  
###  `skip_drop` Â¶

|  `float` |  For booster=dart only: skip_drop (0..1). |  `0.0`  
  
###  `min_child_weight` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as min_rows) Fewest allowed (weighted) observations in a leaf. |  `1.0`  
  
###  `tree_method` Â¶

|  `str` |  Tree method. |  `'auto'`  
  
###  `booster` Â¶

|  `str` |  Booster type. |  `'gbtree'`  
  
###  `col_sample_rate` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as colsample_bylevel) Column sample rate (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `1.0`  
  
###  `max_delta_step` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as max_abs_leafnode_pred) Maximum absolute value of a leaf node prediction. |  `0.0`  
  
###  `max_leaves` Â¶

|  `int` |  For tree_method=hist only: maximum number of leaves. |  `0`  
  
###  `reg_lambda` Â¶

|  `float` |  L2 regularization. |  `1.0`  
  
###  `quiet_mode` Â¶

|  `bool` |  Enable quiet mode. |  `True`  
  
###  `backend` Â¶

|  `str` |  Backend. By default (auto), a GPU is used if available.. |  `'auto'`  
  
###  `nthread` Â¶

|  `int | None` |  Number of parallel threads that can be used to run XGBoost. Cannot exceed H2O cluster limits (-nthreads parameter). Defaults to maximum available. |  `None`  
  
###  `min_data_in_leaf` Â¶

|  `float` |  For tree_method=hist only: the minimum data in a leaf to keep splitting. |  `0.0`  
  
###  `rate_drop` Â¶

|  `float` |  For booster=dart only: rate_drop (0..1). |  `0.0`  
  
###  `min_rows` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as min_child_weight) Fewest allowed (weighted) observations in a leaf. |  `1.0`  
  
###  `col_sample_rate_per_tree` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as colsample_bytree) Column sample rate per tree (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `1.0`  
  
###  `ntrees` Â¶

|  `int` |  (same as n_estimators) Number of trees. |  `50`  
  
###  `gpu_id` Â¶

|  `int` |  Which GPU to use. |  `0`  
  
###  `learn_rate` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as eta) Learning rate (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `0.3`  
  
###  `colsample_bytree` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as col_sample_rate_per_tree) Column sample rate per tree (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `1.0`  
  
###  `min_split_improvement` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as gamma) Minimum relative improvement in squared error reduction for a split to happen. |  `0.0`  
  
###  `sample_rate` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as subsample) Row sample rate per tree (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `1.0`  
  
###  `eta` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as learn_rate) Learning rate (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `0.3`  
  
###  `sub_sample` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as sample_rate) Row sample rate per tree (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `1.0`  
  
###  `max_abs_leafnode_pred` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as max_delta_step) Maximum absolute value of a leaf node prediction. |  `0.0`  
  
###  `gamma` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as min_split_improvement) Minimum relative improvement in squared error reduction for a split to happen. |  `0.0`  
  
###  `max_depth` Â¶

|  `int` |  Maximum tree depth. |  `6`  
  
###  `colsample_bylevel` Â¶

|  `float` |  (same as col_sample_rate) Column sample rate (from 0.0 to 1.0). |  `1.0`  
  
###  `score_tree_interval` Â¶

|  `int` |  Score the model after every so many trees. Disabled if set to 0. |  `0`  
  
###  `normalize_type` Â¶

|  `str` |  For booster=dart only: normalize_type. |  `'tree'`  
  
###  `min_sum_hessian_in_leaf` Â¶

|  `float` |  For tree_method=hist only: the minimum sum of hessian in a leaf to keep splitting. |  `100.0`  
  
###  `grow_policy` Â¶

|  `str` |  Grow policy - depthwise is standard GBM, lossguide is LightGBM. |  `'depthwise'`  
  
###  `one_drop` Â¶

|  `bool` |  For booster=dart only: one_drop. |  `False`  
  
###  `sample_type` Â¶

|  `str` |  For booster=dart only: sample_type. |  `'uniform'`  
  
###  `build_tree_one_node` Â¶

|  `bool` |  Run on one node only. |  `False`  
  
###  `undersampling` Â¶

|  `float` |  The value for undersampling. Only this or oversampling can be used. |  `None`  
  
###  `oversampling` Â¶

|  `float` |  The value for oversampling. Only this or undersampling can be used. |  `None`  
  
###  `seed` Â¶

|  `int` |  The seed value. |  `None`  
  
###  `ignored_fields` Â¶

|  `set of str` |  Set of names corresponding to the fields to be ignored when training a model. |  `None`  
  
###  `schema` Â¶

|  `[Schema](../../schema/#ds_api.schema.Schema "            Schema \(ds_api.schema.Schema\)")` |  Pass a schema to enable conversion between descs and internal names. By not passing a schema, ds-api will assume field names are references to internal Pulse names, not the ones in the UI. |  `None`
